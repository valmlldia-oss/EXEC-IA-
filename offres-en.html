Public funding (France)

For SMEs and mid-market companies with 10 to 2,000 employees based in France, several Bpifrance programmes under France 2030 can reduce the cost of a first AI project: IA Booster offers free awareness sessions, then a Diag Data IA co-funded at 40% (€10,000 excl. tax, €6,000 remaining). The France Num portal lists these schemes.

These programmes fund a technical diagnostic run by a Bpifrance-approved expert, not the upstream strategic trade-off an AI Compass session addresses (which use case to prioritise), nor the deployment of the use cases it identifies (the role EXEC'IA's AI Agents play). The regulatory framework (AI Act, GDPR) is covered in the AI Act and GDPR guide for an SME.
