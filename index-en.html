Why AI isn’t enough

Few AI projects fail for technical reasons. Most lose focus, for lack of priorities and clear decisions.

  • Poorly prioritised use caseschosen without connection to strategic challenges.
  • Experiments without impactpilots that lead to no decision.
  • Poorly aligned teamsbusiness, IT and leadership move out of step.
  • No clear decision frameworkno one decides, trade-offs stall.
  • Decisions endured, not madetechnology sets the pace, not strategy.

Without clear priorities or leadership decisions,
AI remains a cost — never a lever.
