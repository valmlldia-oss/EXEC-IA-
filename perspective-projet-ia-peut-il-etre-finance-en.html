Can your AI project be funded? Yes, but not necessarily the one you have in mind.

Expanded version of a LinkedIn post · 25 September 2026

Short version →

Yes, an AI project can be funded, but not necessarily yours, not for every company and not every service. The Diag Data IA scheme funds a technical analysis carried out by an approved expert. The Osez l’IA catalogue lists ready-to-use solutions, not strategic consulting. A subsidy should never determine your project: first choose the problem to solve, then look at what support applies.

It all started with an audit of my site’s visibility on Google and in AI answers. One question surfaced: what questions do my future clients really ask?

Among them: “Is there any funding for my AI project?” Good question. So I went and looked more closely.

Osez l’IA, France 2030, Bpifrance, Diag Data IA, France Num, regional schemes… The options exist. But behind the word “funding”, the realities differ widely: eligible company or not, fundable service or not, provider freely chosen or selected, funding rate, out-of-pocket cost.

💶 Should funding determine your AI project?

No, never. The order should stay the same: what problem do you want to solve? Where can AI really create value? Which project should be prioritised? And only then: which schemes may apply?

A project built to fit a scheme risks solving no real problem.

What does Diag Data IA fund?

For SMEs and mid-sized companies with 10 to 2,000 employees, Bpifrance’s IA Booster programme, under France 2030, offers free awareness-raising, then a Diag Data IA co-funded at 40%: €10,000 excl. VAT, of which €6,000 excl. VAT is paid by the company. The scheme funds a technical diagnosis carried out by a Bpifrance-approved expert.

The provider is therefore not freely chosen, and the diagnosis does not replace the strategic choice upstream: which use to prioritise, and why that one.

Does “Osez l’IA” fund consulting?

No. The national Osez l’IA plan, run by the French Directorate General for Enterprise and Hub France IA, lists a catalogue of AI solutions for SMEs and mid-sized companies: ready-to-use solutions (assistants, automation, document analysis). It is a product catalogue, not a consulting one.

In practice, a strategic scoping workshop such as Cap IA falls outside the scheme, and no one should imply it could be funded by it. Product solutions, like EXEC’IA’s AI Agents, are of a different nature: they are what this kind of catalogue is meant to list, provided they are selected.

Which questions should you ask before filing an application?

Five checks avoid unpleasant surprises: is your company eligible (size, turnover, sector)? Is the intended service fundable? Can you freely choose your provider, or is it imposed? What is the funding rate? What is the out-of-pocket cost, and for what result?

Precise conditions are published by the bodies themselves (France Num, Bpifrance, entreprises.gouv.fr) and change over time: check them at the source, when you apply.

Where do you start, in practice?

With the problem, not the counter. State what AI should change in your business, identify where it creates value, prioritise a first project. Funding comes afterwards, based on that project.

Funding and compliance should both be anticipated at the scoping stage: for the second, see the AI Act and GDPR guide for an SME.
