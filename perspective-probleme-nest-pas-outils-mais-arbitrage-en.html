More AI tools do not mean more impact. It is not a tools problem. It is a trade-off problem.

Published on LinkedIn · September 9, 2026

If you lead a communications team, you know the reflex: as soon as a new AI tool comes out, you try it. Then another. Then another.

Coordinating communications for the McKinsey France Alumni network, I saw how easy it is to add one more tool, and how rarely anyone stops to ask which one really matters.

It is not a tools problem. It is a trade-off problem.

A Gartner study predicted that 30% of generative AI projects would be abandoned after proof of concept, for lack of clear business value.

🛠️ More AI tools do not mean more impact.
They mean more dispersion, and a team running five initiatives halfway instead of two all the way through.

🎯 The real question is not how many tools to deploy, but which ones, using three criteria:

  1. 1. A real brand, crisis or launch challenge, not a trend to follow?
— 01 —
  1. 2. A committed budget and a named owner ready to decide, not just to watch?
  2. 3. A tool you could switch off tomorrow morning without anyone missing it?

Three initiatives seen through beat fifteen that move in parallel and never arrive.

AI can show you many options. It will never choose for you.

💬 In your communications, how many AI initiatives

are really moving forward, and how many keep spinning without ever finishing?

Source: Gartner, “Predicts 30% of Generative AI Projects Will Be Abandoned After Proof of Concept By End of 2025”, 2024.
