AI transformation rarely fails because of the technology. It fails when the organisation has not changed, only the tool.

Published on LinkedIn · September 9, 2026

During my years alongside senior executives, I saw many tools and transformation plans come and go. AI did not yet exist in its current form. But the script has not changed.

The quality of the tool is rarely the issue. The real subject is what stays the same around it.

A RAND Corporation study now confirms this for AI: more than 80% of projects fail, not for lack of technology, but for lack of alignment with the company’s priorities and organisation.

That figure does not say AI does not work.

It says a tool, on its own, transforms nothing.

🔧 The software changes. The processes stay the same,
the same roles, the same meetings where nobody really decides.

The result: AI is added to what already exists. It does not transform it.

— 01 —

🎯 A real transformation touches three things:

  1. 1. Who decides, and when.
  2. 2. Which processes are rethought, not just automated.
  3. 3. Which roles genuinely evolve.

Without that, the organisation quickly slips back into its old habits, with one more tool.

💬 In your organisation,

what should change before the tool, not after?

Source: RAND Corporation, “The Root Causes of Failure for Artificial Intelligence Projects and How They Can Succeed”, 2024.
