7 AI Opportunities
with High Impact

The use cases that create immediate value — with no technical skills.

🎁 Offered by Valérie Mailland · EXEC'IA Consulting

Most AI projects fail because they start from tools, not needs.

The executives who succeed in their AI transformation are not the most tech-savvy.
They are the ones who knew how to identify the right use cases, at the right time, for the right impact.

Here are the 7 opportunities that produce concrete results — within the first few weeks.

01

Turn every meeting into actionable decisions — automatically

How many meetings end without a clear summary? Without follow-up?

From a simple audio recording or a transcript, AI produces in 30 seconds: a summary of the discussion, decisions made, actions to take with owners and deadlines, and a follow-up email ready to send.

Direct impact: your meetings finally produce measurable results.

⏱ 30 sec to process Meetings Action tracking
02

Walk into a strategic meeting with a complete brief — in 10 minutes

No more meetings where you lack key information when it's time to decide.

AI gathers the context and available data and prepares: a structured agenda, points of attention, strategic questions to ask, and a summary of the latest exchanges with stakeholders.

Direct impact: you enter every meeting with the advantage of preparation.

⏱ 10 min to prepare Decision Leadership
03

Cut writing time by three — without losing quality

Complex emails, minutes, summary notes, executive presentations, commercial proposals — writing absorbs precious time.

AI produces a structured first draft, in the right tone, tailored to your recipient. You review and adjust in seconds. You keep the pen, you gain the time.

Direct impact: 2 to 3 hours recovered per week, per employee.

⏱ -70% writing time Communication Productivity
04

Find the exact information in 10 seconds — in any document

Contracts, procedures, minutes, internal documentation, project histories — your data is there, but finding it takes time.

Ask your question in plain language, as you would a colleague: AI reads, analyses, extracts and summarises the exact information you need.

Direct impact: an end to time-consuming searches, duplicated work and missed information.

⏱ Result in 10 sec Documents Knowledge management
05

Decide with a complete analysis — without waiting for the consultant

Before every important decision, you need a clear view: available options, risks, opportunities, points of attention, precedents.

AI synthesises the available information, compares the scenarios and produces a structured analysis note. You keep the judgement — it gives you the material.

Direct impact: better-informed decisions, made faster, with fewer blind spots.

Strategic analysis Decision Risk reduction
06

Free your teams from repetitive, low-value tasks

Data entry, document filing, client follow-ups, dashboard updates, weekly report generation — these tasks exist in every organisation and consume precious time.

They can be automated. Without coding. Without an IT project. Often in a few days.

Direct impact: your teams focus on what truly creates value.

⏱ ROI in weeks Automation No-code
07

Give your sales teams the advantage of preparation at every meeting

A well-prepared salesperson closes more. AI researches the key information on the prospect, synthesises the sector context, prepares the differentiating arguments and anticipates objections.

It also drafts personalised follow-up emails and keeps the CRM updated without manual entry.

Direct impact: more qualified meetings, better prepared, better followed up — without adding to your teams' workload.
