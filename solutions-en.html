What is the difference between automation and an AI agent?

An automation reacts to a defined event and runs predefined rules in a structured, traceable workflow. An AI agent pursues a goal it is given: it plans and adapts its actions within a defined framework, drawing on tools, data and context, under supervision. Example: booking, CRM update and confirmation sent for automation; preparing an interview for the agent.

Understanding is not enough. The challenge is to move to action.

What is the AI brain, or digital twin?

It is a second brain for executives, with three components: a strategic memory (decisions, trade-offs, documents), up-to-date data from your connected tools, and capacities for action entrusted to AI agents. AI prepares, you decide: your judgment stays in command.

If I share my brain with my teams, am I taking away their know-how?

No. The most useful Skills are the ones each team member writes or adapts themselves: a Skill imposed from above rarely fits how people actually work. You do not take your teams’ know-how. You give them a framework to structure it themselves, in their own way.

Why not wait for Claude or ChatGPT to learn about you on their own?

Anthropic and OpenAI are already building memories that observe how you work. Two limits matter. That memory belongs to the provider: you cannot read it in full or export it, and you depend on one player who sets its prices and rules. And even the best AI tends toward the average answer. A brain built from your own method lets you differentiate, rather than produce what everyone else produces.
